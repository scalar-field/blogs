**Connecting Polymarket on Scalar Field** works differently from every broker connection on the platform. There is no account to link and no OAuth handshake, because Polymarket is an on-chain prediction market. Instead you open a **self-custodied wallet** — Scalar Field never holds your funds — and that wallet becomes the account your agents trade from.

The setup itself takes about a minute and is mostly watching a progress bar. The part that takes longer, and the part people get stuck on, is funding: money has to arrive on the right network, in the right token, in the right wallet before Polymarket will accept an order. This guide covers both, and explains the token and wallet distinctions plainly, because they are the only genuinely confusing thing here.

**What you'll learn:**

* How the Polymarket connection works, and why it creates a wallet instead of linking an account
* What you need before you start — and why paper trading needs none of it
* How to accept the disclosures and open the wallet with an email login
* What each step of the setup queue is actually provisioning
* How to fund the wallet, and what pUSD and the deposit wallet mean
* How orders behave, how to withdraw, how to troubleshoot, and how to disconnect

## How the Polymarket Connection Works

Polymarket markets are binary or multi-outcome events. Their outcome tokens trade between $0.00 and $1.00, so a price reads directly as a probability — a token at $0.36 is the market saying 36%.

To trade them you need a wallet on **Polygon**. Scalar Field creates one for you through **Privy**, a wallet infrastructure provider, using nothing but an email login. The wallet is self-custodied: it is yours, Scalar Field cannot move funds out of it, and it cannot recover it for you either.

One wallet covers three venues. The same setup provisions **Polymarket** on Polygon and **Jupiter** on Solana, and prepares the ground for **Hyperliquid**, which you finish from its own page.

| Specification | Value |
|---|---|
| Instruments | Outcome tokens, identified by `token_id` — there are no tickers |
| Accounts | `POLYMARKET` (live) and `POLYMARKET_PAPER` (simulated) |
| Connection type | Self-custodied wallet, email login through Privy |
| Custody | Yours — Scalar Field never holds or controls funds |
| Network | Polygon |
| Trading currency | pUSD, funded with USDC |
| Hours | 24/7 |
| Fill model | Synchronous — the order blocks until it fills or is rejected |
| Slippage tolerance | `slippage_bps`, default 500 (5%) |
| Minimum order | Market-specific, typically 1–25+ shares; keep order value at or above $1.00 |
| Also activated | Jupiter DEX on Solana, from the same wallet |

**If you only want to test, you can skip this entire guide.** `POLYMARKET_PAPER` is provisioned automatically the first time an agent uses it — no wallet, no funding, no connection. Paper fills are simulated against the live order book with real taker fees, so behavior tracks live closely. Connect the live wallet when you are ready to put real money behind a strategy.

## pUSD and the Deposit Wallet

This is the one genuinely confusing part of Polymarket, and it explains most "my money is there but I can't trade" confusion. Funds pass through two changes before they are tradeable.

**First, the token changes.** You fund with **USDC**, but Polymarket settles in **pUSD**. Converting is one click from your wallet — the platform swaps USDC through bridged **USDC.e** into pUSD, showing each leg as it goes, and it usually takes 15 to 30 seconds. When there is unconverted USDC sitting in the wallet, a banner offers **Convert to pUSD**.

**Second, the wallet changes.** You have two addresses on Polygon:

| | Your wallet (EOA) | Deposit wallet |
|---|---|---|
| What it is | The wallet Privy created for you | The address that places your orders on Polymarket |
| What arrives here | Deposits and conversions | Only what you transfer in |
| Can trade | No | Yes |

Orders are placed by the deposit wallet, so a balance in your own wallet is not yet a balance Polymarket can use. Scalar Field says so directly with a banner:

![The warning banner showing pUSD that is not yet tradeable](https://d32mg6h25qsrpf.cloudfront.net/general_assets/blogs/connect-polymarket-13-pusd-not-tradeable.png)

"$20.00 pUSD in your wallet isn't available for trading yet — Move it into your deposit wallet to trade it on Polymarket." Note the balance at the top of that page still reads $0.00: the headline number tracks what is actually tradeable, not everything you own.

![The transfer banner and its Transfer to deposit wallet button](https://d32mg6h25qsrpf.cloudfront.net/general_assets/blogs/connect-polymarket-14-transfer-banner.png)

Click **Transfer to deposit wallet**.

## How to Withdraw

Click **Withdraw** on the portfolio page.

![The Withdraw Funds modal](https://d32mg6h25qsrpf.cloudfront.net/general_assets/blogs/connect-polymarket-19-withdraw.png)

Pick a token — **USDC**, **USDC.e**, **pUSD**, or **POL**, each showing its balance — then enter a recipient address and an amount. Funds are sent directly from your Polymarket balance and gas is sponsored, so there are no intermediate steps.

Valid destinations are listed in the modal: personal wallets that support Polygon (MetaMask, Coinbase Wallet, Trust Wallet, Rainbow, Phantom), exchange deposit addresses that accept Polygon deposits (Coinbase, Kraken, Binance, Robinhood — selecting "Polygon" as the network), another Scalar Field user's address, or any Polygon `0x` address.

The warning in that modal deserves quoting in full, because self-custody gives you no second chance: "Only send to addresses on the **Polygon** network. Sending to the wrong network means permanent loss of funds. Double-check the recipient address — all transfers are irreversible."

A **$0.20** withdrawal fee is deducted from stablecoin amounts.

## How Orders Behave

Polymarket differs from a brokerage venue in ways that change how you write a strategy.

* **Symbols are outcome token IDs**, not tickers. Take them from the `token_ids` column of `screenPolymarkets()`.
* **Fills are synchronous.** A trade call blocks until it fills or is rejected, on both live and paper accounts.
* **Positions are all-or-nothing.** Every market resolves at its end date, after which positions settle and can no longer be traded. Exit before resolution by policy — a position held through it cannot be managed.
* **Slippage is configurable.** `slippage_bps` defaults to 500, or 5%, which suits thin prediction-market books.
* **Minimums are per market**, typically 1 to 25+ shares. Keep order value at or above $1.00; smaller orders are rejected with `ORDER_BELOW_MINIMUM`.
* **Fractional quantities work.** Size from a dollar budget with `round(budget / price, 2)` — `int()` floors to zero when the budget is below one share's price.
* **Selling can leave dust.** Treat positions under $0.10 of market value as closed.
* **On-chain state lags slightly.** Allow a moment for settlement before re-querying balances after a fill.

```python
from scalarlib import screenPolymarkets, venue

# Find open markets and take an outcome token id
markets = screenPolymarkets(query="bitcoin", closed=False, min_volume=10_000)
token_id = markets.iloc[0]["token_ids"][0]

# Paper account — no wallet or funding required
venue.trade("buy", token_id, 100, account="POLYMARKET_PAPER")

# The same call, against the funded live wallet
venue.trade("buy", token_id, 100, account="POLYMARKET")
```

## Troubleshooting

| What You See | What It Means | What To Do |
|---|---|---|
| "Wallet provider is still loading. Please try again in a moment." | Privy had not initialized when setup started | Wait a moment and click again |
| "Login was cancelled or failed" | The Privy window was closed before the code was accepted | Click **Continue with Email** to restart |
| "Wallet creation timed out. Please try again." | Wallet creation exceeded its time limit | Retry; progress already made is kept |
| "This wallet belongs to a different ScalarField account." | You logged in with an email tied to another account | Click **Continue with Email** again and use the email for this account |
| You closed the modal mid-setup | Setup continues server-side | Watch the "Setting up your wallet..." banner |
| "Polymarket trading could not be fully activated." | The wallet exists but credentials or approvals did not finish | Click **Activate Trading** on the Polymarket card, or finish from the Polymarket page |
| "Solana wallet was not created. Disconnect and reconnect to try again." | The Solana step was skipped, so Jupiter is not active | Polymarket is unaffected; reconnect when you want Jupiter |
| Deposit sent but the balance is still zero | Funds may be on the wrong network or in an unconverted token | Confirm you sent USDC on Polygon; convert to pUSD, then transfer |
| Balance shows but you cannot trade | The funds are in your wallet, not the deposit wallet | Click **Transfer to deposit wallet** |
| "Exceeds available balance" | The amount is larger than the balance for that token | Use **MAX**, or check which token the balance is in |
| "Your wallet session has expired." | The Privy session lapsed during a card purchase | Re-authenticate and retry |
| An order is rejected as below minimum | Under the market's minimum size | Increase the order; keep value at or above $1.00 |
| "After fees, the order size is below the minimum..." | You are selling a position too small to close | Sell more shares, or treat the dust as closed |
| **Cannot Disconnect** | An agent depends on the connection | Pause or delete the listed agents, then disconnect |

## How to Disconnect

Open `scalarfield.io/portfolio/polymarket` and click **Disconnect**.

![The Disconnect Wallet confirmation modal](https://d32mg6h25qsrpf.cloudfront.net/general_assets/blogs/connect-polymarket-20-disconnect.png)

The confirmation is titled **Disconnect Wallet**: "This will remove your wallet connection. Polymarket trading and Jupiter DEX credentials from ScalarField will be deleted. Your on-chain funds and positions are not affected."

That last sentence is the important one. Disconnecting removes Scalar Field's ability to trade for you; it does not touch your money. Your wallet still exists, your balances and positions are still on-chain, and logging in with the same email restores access. Because it takes Jupiter's credentials with it, expect to reconnect if you were using Jupiter too.

One guard applies. If an agent currently depends on the connection, the disconnect is refused with a **Cannot Disconnect** notice listing the agents by name, so nothing breaks silently. Pause or delete them, then disconnect.

## Final Checklist

Before you point an agent at the account, confirm:

* **Wallet:** Setup finished, and **Polymarket (Polygon)** reads **Live**.
* **Access:** You know which email opens the wallet, and that it is the only credential.
* **Funding:** You sent USDC on the Polygon network — not another chain.
* **Token:** Your USDC is converted to pUSD.
* **Wallet placement:** The amount you intend to trade is in the **deposit wallet**, and a **pUSD (tradeable)** tile shows it.
* **Sizing:** You know minimums are per market and order value should be at or above $1.00.
* **Symbols:** Your strategy uses `token_id` values from `screenPolymarkets()`, not tickers.
* **Resolution:** Your strategy exits before markets resolve.
* **Exit:** You know disconnecting removes credentials, not funds.

## Final Thoughts

The wallet setup is the easy half: two checkboxes, an email code, and a progress bar. The half that deserves your attention is the money — right network, right token, right wallet — because self-custody means those decisions are final in a way a broker connection never is.

Start with `POLYMARKET_PAPER`, which costs nothing and needs no wallet, and let a strategy prove itself against live order books first. When you connect the real wallet, fund it with an amount you would be comfortable losing entirely, and transfer to the deposit wallet in tranches rather than all at once. Prediction market positions are all-or-nothing by design, and sizing is the only real control you have over that.

For execution details and the full set of research functions available for prediction market strategies, see the [Polymarket venue documentation](https://scalarfield.io/docs/trading/venues/polymarket).
