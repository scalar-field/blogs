## How the Jupiter Connection Works

There is no Jupiter account. Jupiter is a router: it takes your USDC, finds the best path across Solana's liquidity venues — Raydium, Orca, Meteora, and others — and swaps it for the token you asked for.

That means the connection is really a wallet. Scalar Field creates a Solana wallet through **Privy** using an email login, and that wallet is self-custodied: your funds, your keys, and no ability for Scalar Field to withdraw. It holds USDC and your token positions, and all of it is publicly verifiable on-chain.

The same setup also provisions **Polymarket** on Polygon, since one wallet setup covers both venues.

| Specification | Value |
|---|---|
| Instruments | Any Jupiter-verified Solana token — xStocks, pre-IPO tokens, commodities, index tokens, crypto |
| Symbols | `J:<mint_address>` — a `J:` prefix on the token's mint address |
| Account name in code | `WALLET_SOL` |
| Connection type | Self-custodied Solana wallet, email login through Privy |
| Custody | Yours — Scalar Field can trade but never withdraw |
| Network | Solana |
| Trading currency | USDC in the Solana wallet |
| Hours | 24/7, including tokenized equities |
| Fill model | Synchronous ExactIn swaps |
| Slippage tolerance | `slippage_bps`, default 50 (0.5%) |
| Fractional | Yes |
| Paper mode | None — Jupiter is live only |

The tradeable universe is worth dwelling on, because it is the reason to connect Jupiter at all:

* **Tokenized equities (xStocks)** — tokens like `AAPLx`, `NVDAx`, and `TSLAx`, each backed 1:1 by the underlying security, tradeable around the clock rather than only during US market hours.
* **Pre-IPO tokens** — exposure to SpaceX, OpenAI, Anthropic, and Anduril.
* **Index and commodity tokens** — such as `SPYx`, `QQQx`, `GLDx`, and `SLVx`.
* **Solana crypto** — SOL, JUP, BONK, and any other Jupiter-verified token.

There is no paper mode here. Unlike Polymarket or Alpaca, every Jupiter trade is real, which is worth knowing before you point a new strategy at it.

## If Jupiter Did Not Activate

Two partial states are common enough to be worth naming, and neither requires starting over.

**You have a wallet but no Solana address.** This happens if you connected Polymarket earlier and the Solana step was skipped. Clicking **Set Up Wallet** opens a panel headed **Add Solana execution** — "Polygon custody is live. Add Solana routing to unlock Jupiter trading." Click **Create Solana Wallet**.

**You have a Solana wallet but Jupiter is not activated.** The card reads **Activate Trading**, and the Jupiter page shows **Set Up Jupiter Trading** — "Swap Solana tokens, including tokenized stocks (xStocks), funded by USDC on Solana" — as a two-step checklist:

1. **Fund your Solana wallet.** Buy USDC with a card or send USDC on Solana, via **Fund Wallet**.
2. **Enable trading.** A one-time approval of a Scalar Field trading key for your Solana wallet, described as: "Funds stay in your wallet; the key can trade but never withdraw." Click **Enable Trading**; it takes about ten seconds.

If the wallet setup warned that "Jupiter trading could not be activated. You can retry from the Jupiter portfolio page," this checklist is where you retry.

Keep a little **SOL** on hand for transaction fees, since Solana charges gas in SOL rather than USDC.

## How Orders Behave

Jupiter is a swap venue, not a broker, and three of its quirks will shape your strategy code.

* **Symbols are `J:`-prefixed mint addresses**, like `J:DezXAZ8z7PnrnRJjz3wXBoRgixCa6xjnB7YaB1pPB263`. Plain tickers such as `BONK` are rejected, because lookalike tokens share symbols on Solana. Resolve tokens with `searchJupiterTokens()` or `screenJupiterTokens()`.
* **Swaps are ExactIn.** Your requested quantity is converted at the current price, so `filled_qty` can differ slightly from what you asked for. Read holdings back from `venue.positions()` rather than assuming the request was filled exactly.
* **Fills are synchronous.** The trade call blocks until the swap confirms or fails, and the response carries `tx_hash`, the Solana transaction signature.
* **Slippage defaults to 50 bps** (0.5%), settable per trade. Widen it for thin tokens; the manual **Buy Token** panel exposes the same setting.
* **Everything trades 24/7**, including tokenized equities. There are no market hours to schedule around.
* **There is no order book.** Jupiter aggregates liquidity, so there is a live price but no bid, ask, or depth.
* **Selling can leave dust.** Treat positions under $0.10 of market value as closed.
* **On-chain state lags slightly.** Allow a moment for settlement before re-querying positions after a fill — the sell dialog says as much.

```python
from scalarlib import searchJupiterTokens, venue

# Resolve a token to its J: ticker
tokens = searchJupiterTokens("anthropic")
ticker = tokens.iloc[0]["ticker"]        # 'J:<mint>'

# Buy 5 tokens through the Solana wallet
resp = venue.trade("buy", ticker, 5, account="WALLET_SOL")
print(resp["status"], resp["filled_qty"], resp["avg_price"])

# Sell with a wider slippage tolerance
venue.trade("sell", ticker, 2.5, account="WALLET_SOL", slippage_bps=100)
```

## How to Withdraw

Click **Withdraw** on the Jupiter page.

![The Withdraw Funds modal in Solana mode](https://d32mg6h25qsrpf.cloudfront.net/general_assets/blogs/connect-jupiter-13-withdraw.png)

Choose **USDC** or **SOL**, paste a recipient address, and enter an amount — **MAX** fills in the full balance. Unlike the Polygon side, there is **no withdrawal fee** on Solana for either token.

Valid destinations are listed in the modal: personal wallets that support Solana (Phantom, Solflare, Backpack, Coinbase Wallet), exchange deposit addresses that accept Solana deposits (Coinbase, Kraken, Binance — selecting "Solana" as the network), another Scalar Field user's Solana address, or any base58 Solana public key.

The warning deserves quoting in full, because self-custody offers no recourse: "Only send to addresses on the **Solana** network. Sending to the wrong network means permanent loss of funds. Double-check the recipient address — all transfers are irreversible."

Review, confirm, and **Withdrawal Complete** confirms it was submitted to the Solana network. One practical note: **MAX** on SOL will empty the wallet, and an empty SOL balance leaves nothing to pay gas with, so leave a little behind if you plan to keep trading.

## Troubleshooting

| What You See | What It Means | What To Do |
|---|---|---|
| The card reads **Activate Trading** | The wallet exists but Jupiter's trading key is not registered | Open the Jupiter page and click **Enable Trading** |
| **Add Solana execution** appears | Your wallet has Polygon custody but no Solana wallet | Click **Create Solana Wallet** |
| "Jupiter trading could not be activated." | Activation failed during wallet setup | Retry from the Jupiter page — nothing else is affected |
| "Solana server signer could not be registered..." | The trading key was not registered | Click **Enable Trading** on the Jupiter page |
| "Solana wallet not found. Please set up your wallet with Solana support first." | No Solana address is linked | Run **Add Solana execution** first |
| "Solana wallet was not created. Disconnect and reconnect to try again." | The Solana step was skipped during setup | Reconnect the wallet; Polymarket is unaffected |
| "Failed to enable trading" | Activation call failed | Try again; it is a one-time registration |
| "No tokens found" | The token search matched nothing | Search by name or paste the mint address |
| "Trade failed" or "Sell failed" | The swap was rejected or did not confirm | Check USDC balance, keep some SOL for gas, and widen slippage on thin tokens |
| A fill is smaller than requested | Expected — swaps are ExactIn | Read actual holdings from positions |
| Positions look stale right after a trade | On-chain settlement takes a moment | Use refresh, or check the transaction on Solscan |
| Deposit sent but the balance is zero | Wrong network, or non-SPL USDC | Confirm you sent SPL USDC on Solana |
| **Cannot Disconnect** | An agent depends on the connection | Pause or delete the listed agents, then disconnect |

## How to Disconnect

Click **Disconnect** on the Jupiter page.

![The Disconnect Wallet confirmation modal](https://d32mg6h25qsrpf.cloudfront.net/general_assets/blogs/connect-jupiter-14-disconnect.png)

The confirmation is titled **Disconnect Wallet**: "This will remove your wallet connection. Polymarket trading and Jupiter DEX credentials from ScalarField will be deleted. Your on-chain funds and positions are not affected."

Read that first sentence carefully, because it is easy to misread as Jupiter-only. **Disconnecting here disconnects the whole wallet**, so Polymarket goes with it. There is no way to detach Jupiter alone — it is one wallet, and this removes Scalar Field's access to all of it.

Your money is not affected. The wallet still exists, balances and positions remain on-chain and visible on Solscan, and logging back in with the same email restores access.

If an agent currently depends on the connection, the disconnect is refused with a **Cannot Disconnect** notice listing the agents by name. Pause or delete them, then disconnect.

## Final Checklist

Before you point an agent at the account, confirm:

* **Wallet:** Setup finished, and **Jupiter (Solana)** reads **Live**.
* **Activation:** The Jupiter page shows the portfolio dashboard, not the **Set Up Jupiter Trading** checklist.
* **Access:** You know which email opens the wallet, and that it is the only credential.
* **Funding:** You sent SPL USDC on the Solana network, and it shows in the **USDC** tile.
* **Gas:** A small SOL balance is available for transaction fees.
* **Symbols:** Your strategy resolves `J:` mint addresses rather than passing plain tickers.
* **Slippage:** You have set `slippage_bps` deliberately for the liquidity you are trading.
* **Live only:** You understand there is no Jupiter paper mode, so first trades should be small.
* **Exit:** You know disconnecting removes Polymarket too, but never touches funds.

## Final Thoughts

Jupiter is the most open venue on Scalar Field and the least forgiving. Open, because a strategy can hold tokenized Nvidia, SpaceX exposure, and SOL in one wallet and rebalance at midnight. Unforgiving, because there is no paper mode, no order book to hide behind, and no one to reverse a bad swap.

Handle that with sizing and slippage rather than caution about the connection itself. Fund the wallet with an amount you would accept losing, keep slippage tight on liquid tokens and deliberately wide on thin ones, and let the first few swaps be small enough that a surprising fill is a lesson rather than a loss. Every trade lands on-chain with a transaction hash, so verification is always available — use it early, while you are still learning what your agent actually does.
